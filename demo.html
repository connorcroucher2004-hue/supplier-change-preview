What this beta does — and doesn't

Checks CSV content, validates GTIN checksum and duplicate identifiers, preserves supplier sources in reports, prepares non-published product drafts and formats provided specifications into descriptions. It does not validate legal compliance, check whether image links actually load, verify suppliers, choose sale prices, call an AI model, or connect to Shopify. This is an initial product prototype, not a production SaaS platform.

For existing Shopify products, do not import this file as an overwrite: modifying product variants can destroy variant IDs. This release is for preparing new draft products only.
