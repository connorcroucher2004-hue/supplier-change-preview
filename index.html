How the prototype works

Three steps, zero store access

A practical tool for teams that receive revised CSV files from wholesalers and manufacturers. The private beta is intentionally limited so results can be checked.

01 / LOAD

Provide two CSV files

Choose an earlier and a later supplier snapshot. Or try two included synthetic sample files instead.

02 / COMPARE

Review SKU differences

See changed, added, unchanged and missing-from-new-file records. Duplicate SKU keys are flagged separately.

03 / DECIDE

Export the change report

Download a reviewable CSV or JSON file. A human must confirm changes before importing anything.

What it does today

  • Compares two CSV/TSV supplier snapshots
  • Shows missing, changed and new SKUs
  • Identifies ambiguous duplicate SKUs
  • Exports change reports
  • Also includes a basic new-product CSV audit

What it cannot do yet

  • No Excel .xlsx, PDF or automated supplier feed support
  • No Shopify sync or automatic product editing
  • No SKU-to-variant reconciliation
  • No cost, margin or tax calculation
  • No claim of legal/supplier data accuracy
