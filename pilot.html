What's included

  • One supplier's earlier and newer CSV versions
  • Up to 100 uniquely identified product rows per file
  • Report of changed fields, new SKUs, missing-from-latest-file SKUs and duplicate identifiers
  • Review notes identifying possible issues and a CSV report for your team
  • One round of clarification about the report

What isn't included

  • Automatic updates to your live store
  • Shopify integration, Excel/PDF conversion or supplier API ingestion
  • Guaranteed accuracy of third-party supplier data or price suitability
  • Product research, new descriptions, image matching or stock checks
  • Open-ended cleanup or ongoing subscription
How to request the pilot: If you've received an invitation to test this preview, reply to that email with your approximate number of SKUs, two source file formats and how often suppliers issue updates.
